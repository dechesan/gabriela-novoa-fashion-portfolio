HELLO / I'M GABRIELA

Curiosity is where
my work begins.

I'm a Bachelor of Fashion Design student at LaSalle College Vancouver, with a growing focus on creative research, trend forecasting, colour storytelling and concept development.

I enjoy finding the connections between culture, personal identity, fashion and visual expression. My projects have taken me from a womenswear forecast exploring heritage and innovation, to an emotionally driven illustration concept, an experimental fashion editorial, digital garments and costume breakdown.

I bring an analytical approach to creative work: asking why something resonates, how it communicates and what it could become. My professional experience in hospitality has also shaped the way I collaborate, communicate and respond to challenges.

I'm interested in opportunities where I can learn from experienced creative teams and contribute original ideas to fashion research, editorial production, costume design and creative development.
